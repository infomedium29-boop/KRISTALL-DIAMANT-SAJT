How we calculate the price

A clear price based on your space.

The price depends on the area and volume of the space. We assess the following ten criteria together with the client on a scale from 0 to 10:

  1. 01Degree of soiling
  2. 02How deeply dirt has penetrated the surfaces
  3. 03How crowded the space is with belongings and furniture
  4. 04Selected cleaning option or package
  5. 05Scope of cleaning
  6. 06Complexity of cleaning
  7. 07Additional wishes and requirements
  8. 08Number of cleaning visits per month
  9. 09Number of team members required
  10. 10Type of surfaces

These criteria affect the final price. The final amount is agreed and confirmed after an assessment.
