01 / EXPRESS

EXPRESS

This option is intended for quick maintenance without extensive detailed cleaning. Excellent value for money.

  • We remove dust from visible, open surfaces within arm’s reach, up to approximately 1.80 m.
  • We remove cobwebs throughout the apartment.
  • We wipe door handles and the areas around them, sockets and switches.
  • We vacuum upholstered furniture, carpets and throws.
  • We vacuum and wash floors and wipe skirting boards.
  • We clean kitchen exteriors: cabinet fronts, sink, taps and kitchen wall tiles, removing limescale and grease.
  • We wipe accessible exteriors of medium and large household appliances and the spaces where waste bins stand.
  • In bathrooms and toilets we wash the toilet bowl, the exterior of the washbasin, taps, chrome and nickel surfaces and all visible surfaces, removing limescale, grease and other dirt.
  • Bathrooms, toilets and kitchens are cleaned thoroughly within the EXPRESS service.
02 / ŠPAR

ŠPAR

This is EXPRESS cleaning plus additional cleaning from our list, or another task according to your wishes. We offer 33 additional tasks below.

Every item is cleaned thoroughly. We take over the sequence and clean to our plan. One item may take one or two appointments.

View all 33 additional services ↓
03 / ARGENTUM

ARGENTUM

This cleaning includes everything in EXPRESS, covers more surfaces and goes deeper into hidden and hard-to-reach places.

  • Everything in EXPRESS with approximately 1.5 times more surfaces of the apartment covered, cleaned in greater detail and more intensively.
  • We actively look for dirt, grease and limescale.
  • We include hidden, less visible and some internal surfaces, as well as hard-to-reach places: under beds, behind furniture and in corners.
  • Before wiping a surface we move everything from it, then put the items back.
  • We change bed linen and wash dishes at every visit.
  • We help with food preparation: peeling ingredients and getting them ready for cooking.
  • We also use ladders to clean areas that ordinary cleaning does not cover.
  • Rather than washing the floor only once, we wash it two or three times and disinfect it.
  • We also disinfect the red zones in the kitchen, bathroom and toilet.
  • The aim is a clean, disinfected apartment, down to almost every corner.
  • We agree all details when we visit for a free assessment.

PLATINUM

This option includes cleaning according to the ARGENTUM package plus additional services from the list of 33 tasks, agreed with you.
