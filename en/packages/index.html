The table summarises the owner’s existing texts. The final scope is always confirmed in the quotation and agreement because not all materials, spaces and requirements are the same.

Example of the difference in the bathroom

In the ŠPAR option, limescale product is applied and, after it has worked, removed from the surface as part of regular maintenance. ARGENTUM includes more detailed mechanical work where it is safe for the material — for example, additional rubbing of softened limescale on suitable glass, ceramic or porcelain surfaces.

Example of the difference for dust and items on surfaces

ŠPAR focuses on visible and accessible surfaces. If a table is crowded with objects, the client is expected to clear the area they want cleaned. With ARGENTUM, we can move smaller items and return them to place, and occasionally reach harder-to-access areas.

What does PLATINUM mean?

PLATINUM is not simply “a little more cleaning”. The idea is for the client to think as little as possible about periodic detailed tasks. The plan is defined around the space: interiors, high areas, doors and frames, periodic window cleaning and other detailed tasks are scheduled as agreed.
