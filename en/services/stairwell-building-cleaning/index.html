What can you expect?

We maintain stairwells and common areas of buildings in Zagreb and the surrounding area. Frequency, number of floors, lift and additional areas determine the actual scope of service.

Pricing depends on the number of floors, visit frequency, lift and additional shared areas. Request a quotation for an exact price.
Scope of service

We agree what your space actually needs.

  • sweeping and washing staircases
  • cleaning the entrance area
  • wiping handrails
  • mailboxes
  • fire-extinguisher cabinets
  • window sills and switches
  • area in front of the entrance
  • lift and mirror
  • entrance doors
  • periodic window cleaning as agreed

What is included in the regular plan?

Sweeping and washing staircases and entrances, handrails, mailboxes and fire-extinguisher cabinets, entrance doors, window sills, switches, the area in front of the entrance, plus the inside and outside of the lift and the mirror inside it. Window cleaning can be scheduled periodically.

Working with buildings.

For buildings with regular visits, we define scope and frequency in a quotation or contract, including any additional items important to the residents’ representative or property manager.
